Our games and apps

This section explains how our games, including Mystic Runner, handle information. It applies in addition to the rest of this Privacy Policy.

No accounts. Our games do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. They do not offer chat or other ways to share personal information with other players.

The age question. When you first open the game, it asks your age in a neutral way. Your answer is kept on your device. We use it only to decide how the game treats you: whether you are under 13, 13 to 15, or 16 or over. Only that age band (minor or adult) is shared with our analytics provider. Nothing is sent to our analytics or advertising providers before the question is answered.

Information kept on your device. Your progress, in-game coins and unlocks, settings and recordings of your recent runs are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Deleting the game deletes them, unless your device's own backup keeps a copy.

Information collected automatically. When you play, our analytics provider receives game activity data and device data (described above), including an identifier for the app installation on your device and your approximate region, derived from your IP address. Our games do not collect your device's advertising identifier for analytics, and analytics data is not used for advertising. For players 13 and over, our attribution provider also learns which of our ad campaigns, if any, led to the install (see Third-party services in our games).

Rewarded ads. Some games let you choose to watch a video ad for an in-game reward, such as continuing a run. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one. How ads are chosen depends on your age:

  • Under 13: ads are child-directed, non-personalized and limited to content suitable for all ages. No advertising identifier is used.

  • 13 to 15: ads are non-personalized and treated as for users under the age of consent, with content suitable for teens.

  • 16 and over: where the law requires it (for example in Europe and in some U.S. states), our advertising provider asks for your choices before ads are shown, and you can change them at any time from Privacy Options in the game's settings. Elsewhere, ads may be personalized using your device's advertising identifier, which you can reset or limit in your device settings. On iOS, the game asks whether you allow tracking, and Apple's advertising identifier is used only if you allow it; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

In-game currency. Coins and other items in our games are earned by playing, and some games also sell packs of in-game coins. Purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which take the payment under their own terms and privacy policies; we never receive your card or payment details. We receive only a record of the purchase (the product, the store's transaction identifier and whether it succeeded) so we can deliver the coins. A player under 13, or one who did not answer the age question, cannot start a purchase until a parent passes a check in the game.

Third-party services in our games

Our games use the following third-party services. Each acts as our service provider and processes information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC), to understand how the game is played and to improve it: game activity data, device data, an app-installation identifier and approximate region. Data is kept for up to 14 months. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC), to show rewarded ads: device data, IP address and, for players 16 and over only where allowed, the device's advertising identifier. AdMob is certified under Google Play's Families Ads Program. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • AppsFlyer (AppsFlyer Ltd.), to measure which of our advertising campaigns brought a player to the game (install attribution): device data, IP address, an app-installation identifier, install and launch events and, for players 16 and over, the device's advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking when the game asks). It never runs for players under 13 or before the age question is answered, and for players 13 to 15 it runs without any advertising identifier. On iOS, players 16 and over are asked whether they allow tracking; players who do not allow it are measured only through Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork. See AppsFlyer's Privacy Policy.

  • Apple App Store and Google Play (Apple Inc., Google LLC), to sell packs of in-game coins: they take the payment and handle your payment details under their own privacy policies. We receive only a record of each purchase.

We do not allow these services to collect information from players under 13 for advertising profiles or for any purpose other than providing their service to us.
